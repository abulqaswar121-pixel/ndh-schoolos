# School Owner / Principal

Version 5 · English · Runtime **1:32**

**Audience:** The school owner configuring branding, school accounts, programmes, payroll and reporting.

**Status:** Narrated illustrated release. Scene cuts follow measured paragraph pauses in the selected audio; captions are conformed within each spoken scene. These are illustrated guides, not private-school screen recordings.

## Voice direction

Selected clear English educational voice; not a verified Nigerian-English accent. The following words match the included narration audio. Read CA as “C A”, A4 as “A four”, PIN as “pin”, and Qur’an, Tahfeez, Mu’allim, Halqah, Mumtaz and Jayyid naturally. Pause for taps, offline/save indicators and full-page print checks. Do not read headings or screen callouts aloud.

### 0:00–0:14 · Your own school, your own link

Welcome to NDH SchoolOS, where your school’s short link opens its own crest, motto and colours, for example, ndhschoolos dot app, slash s, slash markaz.

### 0:14–0:21 · Owner email and password

Owners use email and password, while staff and parents use a registered phone number and four-digit PIN, shared privately.

### 0:21–0:28 · Set and share your school identity

Save your school branding and short link, then copy the link for staff and families to open the right school page.

### 0:28–0:37 · Install the school’s own app

Tap Install school app for your school’s crest and name on the home screen, or use Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen on iPhone.

### 0:37–0:45 · Teaching roles and one login

Give dual-role teachers one login for My Class and My Subjects, with only their assigned home class, Halqah and subjects.

### 0:45–0:54 · Everyone belongs on payroll, not in a teacher portal

Payroll separates teaching staff from nannies, security guards, cleaners, drivers and cooks, without unnecessary teacher logins.

### 0:54–1:05 · Keep Tahfeez optional

Conventional schools keep academic terms, while Islamic or dual-stream schools can enable Tahfeez, Halqahs, Surah and Ayah logs, with Mumtaz or Jayyid ratings.

### 1:05–1:15 · Offline work, then automatic sync

After online sign-in, teachers and Mu’allims can draft attendance, CA and exam scores, and memorization offline, then sync automatically when reconnected.

### 1:15–1:23 · Exact A4 reports on every screen

Report cards stay fixed A4 on phone, tablet and computer, so check the complete page and margins before printing.

### 1:23–1:32 · Share the right link and confirm readiness

Before sharing access, test each role, school-app installation, offline sync and A4 printing, keeping PINs and school records private.
