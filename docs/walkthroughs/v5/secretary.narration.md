# School Secretary

Version 5 · English · Runtime **1:11**

**Audience:** The authorised secretary handling admissions, guardian records, notices and permitted printing.

**Status:** Narrated illustrated release. Scene cuts follow measured paragraph pauses in the selected audio; captions are conformed within each spoken scene. These are illustrated guides, not private-school screen recordings.

## Voice direction

Selected clear English educational voice; not a verified Nigerian-English accent. The following words match the included narration audio. Read CA as “C A”, A4 as “A four”, PIN as “pin”, and Qur’an, Tahfeez, Mu’allim, Halqah, Mumtaz and Jayyid naturally. Pause for taps, offline/save indicators and full-page print checks. Do not read headings or screen callouts aloud.

### 0:00–0:10 · Your school’s front office

Welcome, School Secretary, share your school’s own short link, with its crest, motto and colours, rather than a generic school-code login.

### 0:10–0:20 · Phone and PIN for staff and parents

Staff and parents use a registered phone number and four-digit PIN, while only the owner uses email and password, and guardians see linked children only.

### 0:20–0:29 · Help families install their school app

Help families tap Install school app for the school’s own crest and name, or use Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen on iPhone.

### 0:29–0:38 · Keep learners and guardian links accurate

Check admissions, guardian details, sibling links and class transfers carefully, keeping learners with the correct authorised roster.

### 0:38–0:49 · Use the school’s chosen terms

Conventional schools keep academic terms, while Islamic or dual-stream schools can enable Tahfeez and Halqahs, with dual-role teachers using My Class and My Subjects under one login.

### 0:49–1:01 · Coordinate after offline lessons

Teachers and Mu’allims draft attendance, scores and memorization offline after online setup, then sync automatically on reconnect, so wait for confirmation before front-office follow-up.

### 1:01–1:11 · Print complete A4 report cards

Print only authorised reports, checking that the same fixed A4 page scales across phone, tablet and computer with no clipped print margins.
