# Parent / Guardian

Version 5 · English · Runtime **1:06**

**Audience:** A parent or guardian using only their school account and linked children’s information.

**Status:** Narrated illustrated release. Scene cuts follow measured paragraph pauses in the selected audio; captions are conformed within each spoken scene. These are illustrated guides, not private-school screen recordings.

## Voice direction

Selected clear English educational voice; not a verified Nigerian-English accent. The following words match the included narration audio. Read CA as “C A”, A4 as “A four”, PIN as “pin”, and Qur’an, Tahfeez, Mu’allim, Halqah, Mumtaz and Jayyid naturally. Pause for taps, offline/save indicators and full-page print checks. Do not read headings or screen callouts aloud.

### 0:00–0:13 · Your child’s school, straight from its link

Welcome, Parent or Guardian, open your school’s short link, for example, ndhschoolos dot app, slash s, slash markaz, and check its crest, motto and colours.

### 0:13–0:22 · Your phone number and four-digit PIN

Use your registered phone number and privately supplied four-digit PIN, with no email or password needed, and contact your school if you need help.

### 0:22–0:31 · Keep your school app on your phone

Tap Install school app for the school crest and name on your home screen, or use Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen on iPhone.

### 0:31–0:38 · Only your linked children

Choose from your linked children to check attendance and school updates, asking the school to correct any missing or incorrect child link.

### 0:38–0:47 · Published results and exact A4 reports

Open published results and the same fixed A4 report on phone, tablet or computer, then check the full page and margins before printing.

### 0:47–0:57 · Understand updates from lessons

Teachers save lesson records offline and sync automatically on reconnect, so updates follow sync and school release rules, with Tahfeez optional for enabled schools.

### 0:57–1:06 · Fees, verified receipts and safe use

Check your child’s fees and official receipts, remember that new sign-ins, online payments and messages need internet, and keep your PIN private.
