# School Owner / Principal — screen tutorial

Version 6 · Phone + Desktop · 2:35

Actual application components with fictional demo records. Not live authentication, native installation, bank verification or physical printing. Selected English voice-02, not a verified Nigerian accent.

## 0:00 · Homepage → Register

Welcome. These are the actual NDH SchoolOS pages, with fictional demo records. Start on the homepage and choose Register Your School. On a phone, scroll to the button. No real school accounts are involved.

## 0:17 · School details

Enter your school name, country and address. Upload your own school crest if available. Check the details, then choose Next. We are using a fictional training school, not registering a real school.

## 0:32 · Owner account & classes

Enter the owner's name, administrator email and a strong password. Keep your real password private. Select Next to review your session, term, programmes and classes. Complete registration on the live service and follow any email confirmation instructions. We will not create a real account in this demonstration.

## 0:55 · Sign in & dashboard

For an existing owner account, choose Sign In and enter your email and password. Staff and parents use their school-provisioned phone and four-digit PIN. This local demo session shows the dashboard, not live authentication.

## 1:12 · Settings & school link

On a phone, open the navigation menu. On desktop, use the sidebar. Choose Settings to review your crest, name and motto. Scroll to School portal and accounts for the short link. Share that link, not your owner login.

## 1:29 · Teaching & support staff

Open Staff and Payroll, then Staff Directory. Filter teaching and non-teaching staff. Support staff can be paid without teaching accounts. Review a teacher's class and subject assignments in one staff record.

## 1:44 · Optional Quran module

Leave the optional Quran module off for a conventional school, or enable it when your school needs memorization tracking.

## 1:53 · Real A4 report preview

Return to the dashboard and choose a learner's Report Card. This opens the actual A four print preview. On a phone the same paper scales down; the report itself does not rearrange. Check the full page and margins before printing or saving to PDF.

## 2:12 · School app & offline guidance

Open your school's link and choose Install school app. The help explains the Safari and Chrome steps. Sign in online and open needed records before working offline. Reconnect with the app open and review sync or conflict messages. Installation and live offline sync are not being verified in this demo.
