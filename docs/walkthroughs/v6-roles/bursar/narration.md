# Bursar / Accountant — screen tutorial

Version 6 · Phone + Desktop · 1:42

Actual application components with fictional demo records. Not live authentication, native installation, bank verification or physical printing. Selected English voice-02, not a verified Nigerian accent.

## 0:00 · School link & sign-in

Open your school's branded link. Staff and parents use their registered phone and four-digit PIN. This recording uses fictional demo records and a local session, not live authentication.

## 0:13 · Student balances & debtors

Choose Fees and Receipts. Review Student Balances and Debtors, then filter owing accounts. Confirm actual bank transfers before issuing official receipts; the figures here are fictional.

## 0:28 · Official receipt → A4 preview

Open Receipts History and inspect the demo receipt. Use the receipt preview to check the details before printing on A four paper. Review the learner, amount and receipt number before printing.

## 0:43 · Monthly payroll & payslips

Open Staff and Payroll. Select the month and review the salary rows. Open the demo payslip to check additions, deductions and net salary before payment.

## 0:55 · Teaching & non-teaching staff

Open Staff and Role Assignments and filter non-teaching staff. Nannies, security, cleaners, drivers and cooks belong on payroll without unnecessary teaching logins.

## 1:09 · Install school app

Open your school's link and choose Install school app. The real help dialog explains Safari and Chrome steps. A native installation is not being verified here.

## 1:21 · Online payments & sync limits

Sign in online and open needed records before offline work. Attendance and score drafts can sync when reconnected; review conflicts. Payments, messages and new sign-ins need internet. This demo does not verify live sync. A locally shown balance is not bank verification.
