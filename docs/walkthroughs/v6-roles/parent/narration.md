Actual application components with fictional demo records. Not live authentication, native installation, bank verification or physical printing. Selected English voice-02, not a verified Nigerian accent.

## 0:00 · School link & parent sign-in

Open your school's branded link. Staff and parents use their registered phone and four-digit PIN. This recording uses fictional demo records and a local session, not live authentication.

## 0:14 · Linked children & attendance

The Parent Portal shows only your linked children. Select a sibling and review the chosen term and attendance. These demo children are fictional; another family is excluded from this view.

## 0:28 · Published result → A4 report

Select Print Report Card for a published result. Inspect the actual fixed A four page before printing or saving to PDF. Parents do not enter scores or see unpublished results.

## 0:42 · Fees, receipts & transfer safety

Scroll to the child fee summary and official receipts. Inspect the demo receipt. Confirm payment instructions with the school; a transfer notice is not proof that money was received.

## 0:56 · Install your school app

Open your school's link and choose Install school app. The real help dialog explains Safari and Chrome steps. A native installation is not being verified here.

## 1:09 · Safe offline viewing

Sign in online first and open the records you need on your own device. Offline copies may be out of date until reconnection. New sign-ins, payments and messages need internet. Keep your PIN private; live sync is not being verified here.
