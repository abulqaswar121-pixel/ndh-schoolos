# School Secretary — screen tutorial

Version 6 · Phone + Desktop · 1:26

Actual application components with fictional demo records. Not live authentication, native installation, bank verification or physical printing. Selected English voice-02, not a verified Nigerian accent.

## 0:00 · School link & sign-in

Open your school's branded link. Staff and parents use their registered phone and four-digit PIN. This recording uses fictional demo records and a local session, not live authentication.

## 0:13 · Admissions & guardian links

Choose Students, search the demo roster and open a learner record. Review the admission details and guardian phone. Link siblings carefully; never give one family access to another learner.

## 0:27 · Attendance & authorised classes

Choose Attendance and check the class and date. Review the summary and open the attendance sheet preview. The secretary uses authorised records, not owner settings or teacher score editing.

## 0:41 · Read-only A4 report printing

Return to Students and open a report card. Check the same fixed A four page on either device. Print only reports your role is allowed to access and follow the school publication process.

## 0:54 · School app guidance

Open your school's link and choose Install school app. The real help dialog explains Safari and Chrome steps. A native installation is not being verified here.

## 1:06 · Offline boundaries & privacy

Sign in online and open needed records before offline work. Attendance and score drafts can sync when reconnected; review conflicts. Payments, messages and new sign-ins need internet. This demo does not verify live sync. Keep school links and family records private.
