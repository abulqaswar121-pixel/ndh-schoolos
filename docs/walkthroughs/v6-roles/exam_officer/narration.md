# Exam Officer — screen tutorial

Version 6 · Phone + Desktop · 1:26

Actual application components with fictional demo records. Not live authentication, native installation, bank verification or physical printing. Selected English voice-02, not a verified Nigerian accent.

## 0:00 · School link & sign-in

Open your school's branded link. Staff and parents use their registered phone and four-digit PIN. This recording uses fictional demo records and a local session, not live authentication.

## 0:13 · Find and filter exam papers

Open the menu and choose Exam Questions. Review the missing-paper tracker and filter by term, class or paper status. Check the submitted teacher paper before approving it.

## 0:27 · Review → approve → A4 paper

Open the submitted paper preview and check its questions. Close the preview, then approve the demo paper. The paper and confidential marking scheme have separate PDF and Word controls.

## 0:40 · All-class broadsheets & reports

Choose Results and Report Cards, then Class Broadsheet and Report Cards. Select the class and term. Open a learner report to inspect the actual fixed A four page before printing.

## 0:54 · Install school app

Open your school's link and choose Install school app. The real help dialog explains Safari and Chrome steps. A native installation is not being verified here.

## 1:05 · Offline guidance & privacy

Sign in online and open needed records before offline work. Attendance and score drafts can sync when reconnected; review conflicts. Payments, messages and new sign-ins need internet. This demo does not verify live sync. Keep marking schemes private and publish results only after the school review.
