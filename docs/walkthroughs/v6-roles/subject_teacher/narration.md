# Subject Teacher — screen tutorial

Version 6 · Phone + Desktop · 1:30

Actual application components with fictional demo records. Not live authentication, native installation, bank verification or physical printing. Selected English voice-02, not a verified Nigerian accent.

## 0:00 · School link & sign-in

Open your school's branded link. Staff and parents use their registered phone and four-digit PIN. This recording uses fictional demo records and a local session, not live authentication.

## 0:13 · Assigned subject → score entry

Choose Results and Report Cards. Check the session, term, class and subject before entering scores. We change one fictional learner score here; your real account remains limited to its assignments.

## 0:28 · Combined teaching group

Use Recording Mode to select your assigned combined group. Learners retain their home classes, so scores entered once can feed their class results. Inspect the actual A four marksheet preview.

## 0:43 · Exam paper drafting & submission

Choose Exam Questions and open the demo draft. Review the question and its options before submission. The Exam Officer handles approval; a subject teacher does not gain all-school reporting permissions.

## 0:58 · Install school app

Open your school's link and choose Install school app. The real help dialog explains Safari and Chrome steps. A native installation is not being verified here.

## 1:11 · Offline scores & reconnect

Sign in online and open needed records before offline work. Attendance and score drafts can sync when reconnected; review conflicts. Payments, messages and new sign-ins need internet. This demo does not verify live sync. Keep any unsynced work on your own device.
